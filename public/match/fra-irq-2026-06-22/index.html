Match Preview

Head-to-head History

France and Iraq have never met in an international match. This is a first-time encounter between the two-time world champions and the resilient Asian nation. France has a long history of success against teams from all continents, while Iraq is returning to the World Cup stage for the first time since 1986. The lack of head-to-head history makes this a unique challenge for both coaching staffs, with France entering as massive favorites. Iraq will be looking to make a respectable account of themselves and perhaps pull off the biggest shock in their footballing history.

Form & Ranking

France is ranked 2nd in the world and arrives as one of the top favorites for the title. Their squad is deep and full of world-class talent in every position. Iraq is ranked 65th, having qualified after a strong AFC campaign. France has been in dominant form, winning their last five competitive matches by comfortable margins. Iraq has shown great defensive discipline in their preparation. France has a fully fit squad, while Iraq is missing their starting left-back due to injury. The disparity in quality and experience makes France the overwhelming favorite for this fixture in Philadelphia.

What to Expect

Expect France to dominate possession and launch wave after wave of attacks against a compact Iraqi defense. Iraq will likely play a very defensive game, looking to keep the scoreline respectable and hoping for a rare chance on the counter-attack. Media outlets like L'Equipe and various Middle Eastern sources expect a comfortable 3-0 or 4-0 win for France. The Athletic notes that for France, this match is an opportunity to rotate their squad and build momentum. For Iraq, the goal will be to frustrate the French for as long as possible and show their organizational improvement.
