Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Morocco and Haiti have never met in an international football match. This is a historic first meeting between the North African power and the Caribbean side. Morocco has a long history of success against teams from all regions, particularly since their 2022 breakout, while Haiti is enjoying one of the best periods in their footballing history. The lack of prior history makes this an intriguing tactical introduction for both sides in Atlanta. Morocco enters as heavy favorites, but Haiti’s athleticism and recent improvements mean they will not be taken lightly by the 'Atlas Lions.'

Form & Ranking

Morocco is ranked 13th in the world, the highest-ranked team from Africa. They arrive with a squad full of experience and technical quality. Haiti is ranked 84th, having qualified after a strong CONCACAF performance. Morocco’s form has been excellent, characterized by defensive solidity and quick transitions. Haiti has been competitive in their region but faces a massive step up in quality. Both teams are reporting mostly fit squads, though Morocco is monitoring the fitness of their key playmakers. Morocco is the clear favorite based on quality and recent major tournament pedigree.

What to Expect

Morocco is expected to control the game and use their technical superiority to break down a likely defensive Haitian setup. Haiti will look to stay compact and use their speed on the counter-attack to threaten the Moroccan goal. Analysts at beIN Sports and various African outlets predict a 2-0 or 3-0 win for Morocco, citing the disparity in individual talent. The Athletic notes that for Morocco, this match is vital for securing their place in the knockout rounds. A professional and disciplined performance by the Moroccans is the general expectation from most football pundits.
