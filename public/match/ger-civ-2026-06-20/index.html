Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Germany and Ivory Coast have a very brief history, having met only once in an international friendly in 2009. That match ended in a 2-2 draw, showcasing the competitive nature of the West African giants against European opposition. This World Cup fixture in Toronto is their first-ever competitive meeting. Germany has a long tradition of success against African teams, but Ivory Coast’s physicality and individual talent have historically troubled the Germans. This match serves as a modern-day test for two nations that have not faced each other in over 15 years.

Form & Ranking

Germany is ranked 9th in the world, having enjoyed a successful rebuilding phase under Julian Nagelsmann. Their form has been dominant in Europe, characterized by high-intensity pressing. Ivory Coast is ranked 33rd, the reigning AFCON champions (as of 2024) and one of the most athletic teams in the world. Germany enters the match with a fully fit squad, while Ivory Coast is monitoring the fitness of Sebastian Haller. Germany is the clear favorite, but Ivory Coast’s recent form in major tournaments suggests they are more than capable of holding their own against elite opposition.

What to Expect

Expect Germany to try and dominate the ball and use their technical superiority to open up the Ivorian defense. Ivory Coast will look to use their physical strength and speed in transition to hit Germany on the break. Pundits at Kicker and Canal+ predict a 2-1 win for Germany, but they warn that the Ivorian midfield could cause significant problems for the German buildup. The Athletic highlights the matchup between Jamal Musiala and the Ivorian defensive pivots as the most critical tactical battle. A high-scoring, entertaining game is anticipated by most analysts.
