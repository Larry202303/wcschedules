Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Norway and Senegal have met only once before, in a 2004 friendly match that ended in a 2-2 draw. That match, played over two decades ago, provides little relevant data for the current squads but hints at the high-scoring potential of their encounters. This World Cup fixture in New Jersey is their first-ever competitive meeting. It pits the rising European power of Norway, led by their superstar generation, against the consistent top-tier strength of Senegal, the standard-bearer for African football in recent years. Both teams will be looking to establish a lead in this fresh intercontinental rivalry.

Form & Ranking

Senegal is ranked 15th in the world and has been in excellent form, having won the 2021 AFCON and performed well in the 2022 World Cup. Norway is ranked 30th but is seen as a much bigger threat due to the presence of Erling Haaland and Martin Ødegaard. Senegal has a balanced, experienced squad, while Norway relies heavily on their star individuals. Both teams enter the match with mostly fit squads, though Norway is monitoring a key defender's recovery. Senegal’s greater tournament experience gives them a slight psychological edge in this crucial Group I clash.

What to Expect

This is expected to be a fascinating tactical battle. Norway will look to use Ødegaard’s vision to release Haaland behind the Senegalese defense. Senegal will rely on their physicality and the pace of Sadio Mané and Nicolas Jackson to hit Norway on the break. Pundits at the BBC and various African outlets are predicting a 1-1 draw, noting the evenly matched nature of the two sides. The Athletic highlights that the winner of the duel between Haaland and Senegal’s center-backs will likely decide the match. A high-intensity game with goals for both sides is the general consensus.
