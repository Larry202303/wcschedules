Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Spain and Saudi Arabia have met three times in the past, with Spain winning all three encounters. Their most significant meeting was at the 2006 World Cup, where a heavily rotated Spanish side secured a 1-0 victory. They also met in a 2012 friendly which Spain won 5-0. Spain holds a perfect 3-0-0 record against the Green Falcons with an aggregate score of 9-2. This match in Atlanta is their first competitive meeting in 20 years, and Spain will be looking to continue their dominance while Saudi Arabia aims for a historic first-ever result against La Roja.

Form & Ranking

Spain is ranked 3rd in the world and arrives as one of the tournament favorites after winning Euro 2024. Saudi Arabia is ranked 54th, having continued their development following their shock victory over Argentina in 2022. Spain’s form has been impeccable, winning their last 10 competitive matches. Saudi Arabia has been competitive in Asia but has struggled in recent intercontinental friendlies. Spain enters the match with a fully fit squad. Saudi Arabia is also healthy and will rely on the chemistry of their domestically-based squad. Spain is the overwhelming favorite for this fixture.

What to Expect

Spain is expected to control over 70% of the ball and patiently look for openings in the Saudi defense. Saudi Arabia will likely employ a similar strategy to their 2022 win over Argentina, using a high defensive line and looking to catch Spain off guard on the break. Pundits at Marca and Al Jazeera predict a 2-0 or 3-0 win for Spain, but they warn that Saudi Arabia's tactical discipline has improved significantly. The Athletic highlights that Spain’s ability to convert their dominance into goals will be the key to avoiding a frustrating afternoon.
