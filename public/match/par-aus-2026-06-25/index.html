Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Paraguay and Australia have met only once in an international friendly in 2010, which Australia won 1-0. That match, played over 15 years ago, provides little relevant data for the current squads but marks Australia's 100% winning record in the rivalry. This World Cup fixture in San Francisco is their first-ever competitive meeting. It pits the traditional South American defensive grit of Paraguay against the hard-working, collective style of the modern Australian team. Both nations will be looking to establish a lead in their head-to-head history in this crucial Group D deciding match.

Form & Ranking

Australia is ranked 24th in the world, having become one of Asia's most consistent teams under Graham Arnold. Paraguay is ranked 49th, known for their defensive organization and physical presence in CONMEBOL. Australia’s form has been solid, characterized by their work rate and set-piece strength. Paraguay has been tough to beat but has struggled to score against top-tier opposition. Both teams enter the match with no major injury concerns. The rankings suggest a very close match, with Australia entering as the slight favorite based on their more recent competitive success.

What to Expect

Expect a physical, hard-fought match between two teams that prioritize defensive organization. Australia will look to use their collective work rate and physicality on set-pieces, while Paraguay will likely adopt a similar approach, focusing on defensive discipline and striking on the counter-attack. Pundits at various Australian and South American outlets are predicting a 0-0 or 1-1 draw, noting the similar styles of the two sides. The Athletic highlights that a single goal could easily decide this match, which will be critical for both teams' hopes of advancing to the knockout rounds.
