Match Preview

Head-to-head History

The United States and Paraguay have a competitive history dating back to the inaugural 1930 World Cup, where the USA won 3-0. In more recent years, they have met in the 2016 Copa America Centenario, with the USA winning 1-0. The overall head-to-head record is remarkably balanced, with both teams sharing wins in friendly matches over the decades. This match in Los Angeles will be their first World Cup meeting in nearly a century, renewing a rivalry between two nations with very different footballing identities.

Form & Ranking

The USA enters their home tournament ranked 11th in the world, their highest position in years. Boasting a squad full of European-based talent like Christian Pulisic and Folarin Balogun, they are in excellent form. Paraguay is ranked 49th, having fought through the grueling CONMEBOL qualifiers. Paraguay is known for their 'garra'—a gritty, defensive fighting spirit—and they have recently held their own against giants like Brazil and Argentina. The USA is largely healthy, while Paraguay is missing a key defender due to a late-season ligament injury.

What to Expect

Expect the USA to play with high intensity and look to use the wide areas at SoFi Stadium. Paraguay will likely deploy a 'low block' and rely on set pieces and physical defending to frustrate the Americans. Media outlets like FOX Sports and The Athletic suggest that the USA's talent depth should eventually prevail, but they caution against complacency. Pundits favor a USA win, but many predict a low-scoring affair, such as 1-0 or 2-0. Christian Pulisic’s ability to unlock the Paraguayan defense is seen as the primary key to the match by most tactical analysts.
