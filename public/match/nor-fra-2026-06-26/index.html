Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Norway and France have a long history, having met 15 times. France holds a significant advantage with 7 wins to Norway's 4, with 4 draws. Their most recent significant meeting was a 2014 friendly which France won 4-0. However, Norway secured a 2-1 victory in a 2010 friendly. Their last World Cup meeting was in 1998, which France won 1-0. Historically, France has been the dominant side, but Norway’s current generation is arguably the strongest in their history, making this a highly anticipated modern clash in front of a global audience in Boston.

Form & Ranking

France is ranked 2nd in the world and arrives as a top favorite, with their squad boasting world-class talent like Kylian Mbappé. Norway is ranked 30th but is seen as a major threat due to the presence of Erling Haaland and Martin Ødegaard. France’s form has been dominant, while Norway has been inconsistent but capable of beating top sides. Both teams enter the match with fully fit squads. France is the favorite based on their depth and experience, but the duel between Haaland and the French defenders makes this one of the most exciting matchups of the tournament.

What to Expect

This is expected to be a high-level tactical battle. France will look to control the ball and release their explosive pace in transition. Norway will rely on Martin Ødegaard’s vision to feed Erling Haaland and look to exploit any defensive lapses from the French. Pundits at L'Equipe and various European outlets predict a 2-1 win for France, citing their superior depth in all positions. The Athletic highlights that the winner of the duel between Haaland and the French center-backs will likely decide the match. A high-intensity game with world-class individual talent on display is anticipated.
