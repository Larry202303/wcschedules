Match Preview

Head-to-head History

England and Croatia have developed one of Europe's most intense modern rivalries, having met 11 times. The record is relatively balanced, with England winning 6, Croatia 3, and 2 draws. However, Croatia won the most significant meeting—the 2018 World Cup semi-final (2-1). England got some revenge with a 1-0 win at Euro 2020. Their matches are always tactically fascinating and often decided by thin margins. This match in Dallas is another chapter in a rivalry that has come to define the top tier of European international football.

Form & Ranking

England is ranked 4th in the world and arrives as one of the top favorites with a squad led by Harry Kane and Jude Bellingham. Croatia is ranked 10th, continuing to defy logic with their longevity and technical excellence, still led by the ageless Luka Modrić. England’s form has been dominant in qualifying, while Croatia has navigated their path with their trademark resilience. England has a fully fit squad, while Croatia is managing the fitness of their veteran core. Both teams are very familiar with each other, with many players being teammates at the club level.

What to Expect

Expect a high-level tactical chess match. England will likely look to use their superior pace and depth, while Croatia will aim to control the midfield through Modrić and Kovačić. Pundits at the BBC and Sky Sports suggest England's younger legs and attacking options make them favorites, but they never count out Croatia in a tournament setting. Most experts predict a 1-1 draw or a 2-1 win for England. The Athletic highlights the battle between Bellingham and Modrić as the key individual matchup that could decide the flow of the game.
