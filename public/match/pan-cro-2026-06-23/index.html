Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Panama and Croatia have never met in an international match. This is a brand-new fixture for the World Cup, pitting the technical and resilient Balkan power against the improving Central American side. Croatia has a storied World Cup history, including a final and a semi-final appearance in the last two editions. Panama is appearing in their second tournament and looking to build on their 2018 experience. The lack of prior history means both teams are relying on scouting reports, making this an intriguing tactical introduction for both nations in Toronto.

Form & Ranking

Croatia is ranked 10th in the world, continuing to perform at a high level with their veteran core led by Luka Modrić. Panama is ranked 53rd, having shown significant improvement under Thomas Christiansen. Croatia’s form has been solid, while Panama has been competitive in CONCACAF. Both teams have no major injury concerns, though Croatia is managing the fitness of their aging stars. Croatia enters as the favorite, with their superior technical ability and tournament experience expected to be the deciding factors against a hard-working Panama side.

What to Expect

Croatia is expected to control the tempo of the game through their elite midfield, seeking to wear down the Panamanian defense with their passing. Panama will likely stay compact and look to use their pace on the counter-attack to threaten the Croatian goal. Pundits at ESPN and various European outlets predict a 2-0 win for Croatia, but they warn that Panama’s organization could make it a frustrating game. The Athletic highlights that for Croatia, this match is vital for securing a knockout spot. A professional and disciplined performance by the Croatians is the general expectation.
