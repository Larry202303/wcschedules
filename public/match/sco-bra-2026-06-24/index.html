Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Scotland and Brazil have met 10 times, with Brazil holding a dominant 9-1-0 record. Their most famous encounter was the opening match of the 1998 World Cup, which Brazil won 2-1 after a late Scotland own goal. They also met at the 1982 and 1990 World Cups, with Brazil winning both (4-1 and 1-0). Scotland's only draw against the Seleção came in 1974. Historically, Brazil has been Scotland's ultimate 'bogey team,' and the Tartan Army will be looking to secure their first-ever victory or even their second-ever draw against the five-time champions in Miami.

Form & Ranking

Brazil is ranked 5th in the world and arrives as one of the favorites, with their squad boasting world-class talent like Vinicius Jr. and Rodrygo. Scotland is ranked 44th, having qualified for their first World Cup in 28 years. Brazil’s form has been strong, though they have shown some defensive vulnerabilities in CONMEBOL. Scotland has been incredibly difficult to beat under Steve Clarke but lacks the individual star power of the Brazilians. Both teams enter the match with no major injury concerns, though Scotland's depth will be tested in the Florida heat.

What to Expect

Expect Brazil to dominate possession and showcase their technical flair, while Scotland will look to play a very disciplined, physical, and defensive game. Scotland will rely on set-pieces and the individual work rate of players like Scott McTominay to create chances. Pundits at the BBC and Globo Esporte predict a 2-0 or 3-1 win for Brazil, citing the gulf in quality between the two sides. The Athletic highlights that for Brazil, this is a chance to secure the top spot in the group. Most experts expect Scotland to put up a brave fight, but Brazil’s attacking depth should eventually prevail.
