Match Preview

Head-to-head History

Argentina and Algeria have only met once in a high-scoring and memorable friendly match in 2007, which Argentina won 4-3. That game featured goals from Lionel Messi and showcased the attacking flair of both nations. This World Cup fixture in Kansas City is their first competitive meeting. While the 2007 match was nearly two decades ago, the memory of that open, attacking game has set high expectations for this Group J clash. Argentina leads the head-to-head 1-0, but Algeria’s performance that day proved they could go toe-to-toe with the world's best.

Form & Ranking

Argentina arrives as the world's #1 ranked team and the defending champions (from 2022). They have continued their dominance in South America under Lionel Scaloni. Algeria is ranked 34th, having rebounded strongly from recent AFCON disappointments to qualify for the World Cup. Argentina’s form is impeccable, winning almost every competitive match in the last two years. Algeria has been scoring freely in their qualifiers but faces a massive step up in quality. Argentina has no major injury concerns, while Algeria is hoping their star winger can recover from a late-season knock.

What to Expect

Argentina is expected to dominate possession and dictate the tempo through their technical midfield. Algeria will likely look to play an aggressive, high-pressing game to disrupt Argentina's rhythm. Pundits at TyC Sports and beIN Sports predict a comfortable 2-0 or 3-0 win for Argentina. However, many note that Algeria's individual talent can create chances out of nothing. The Athletic highlights the tactical battle between Scaloni’s structured approach and Algeria’s more flair-based play. The Kansas City crowd is expected to be overwhelmingly pro-Argentina, creating a 'home' atmosphere for the champions.
