Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Welcome to WCSchedules.com ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website at https://www.wcschedules.com.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our website. We may collect the following non-personal information automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website or search engine
  • General geographic location (country/region level, not precise)
  • Language and timezone preferences you select on our site (stored locally in your browser)

2. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your preferences (selected language, timezone, and favorite team). This data is stored entirely on your device and is never sent to our servers.

We may use cookies for analytics and advertising purposes as described below. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

3. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

4. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to visiting. We use this information to improve our website. Google Analytics collects only the IP address assigned to you on the date you visit our site, not your name or other personally identifying information.

You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing you on return visits by disabling cookies or using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website displays data from the following third-party services:

  • Polymarket – We display publicly available prediction market odds. We do not share your data with Polymarket.
  • Vercel – Our website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect server logs including IP addresses for security and performance purposes. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Preferences saved in your browser's localStorage remain on your device until you clear your browser data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • The right to access information we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of personalized advertising
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (EU users: under GDPR)

Since we do not collect personal data directly, most requests can be handled by clearing your browser's localStorage and cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Website: https://www.wcschedules.com
  • Email: privacy@wcschedules.com
